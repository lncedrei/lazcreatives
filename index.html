LAZ CREATIVES

Laz Creatives is a freelance service founded by Lance. It specializes in software development, UI/UX design, and graphic design. Each project is carefully examined and crafted with a focus on purpose, functionality, and design aesthetics. High-quality outputs are provided at reasonable prices, and additional options are also offered that align with clients' budgets.

Previously, It is also known as "War Graphics." This online service gained recognition within the gaming community, boasting over 70 satisfied clients and more than 300 completed projects nationwide. Initially limited to graphic design, War Graphics has now rebranded as Laz Creatives, introducing two new services with enhanced quality.

For more details, you may message @lazcreatives or @lncedrei_ on Instagram.
